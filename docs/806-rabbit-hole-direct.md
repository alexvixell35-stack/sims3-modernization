# 806: `rabbitholedirect`, `rhoutside`, unseen townies enter rabbit holes without the trip (ON since import 2026-11-20)

Answer: two ts3fixes switches (ON since import 2026-11-20) stop EA from planning and walking a whole trip to a rabbit hole (a building such as a restaurant, workplace or shop) for an unseen townie. Household, selected and unknown-scope Sims are never touched, and nothing changes with the switch off.

- `rabbitholedirect` (tuning `kRabbitHoleDirect`): in `RouteNearEntranceAndEnterRabbitHole` an unseen townie is placed on one of the building's enter slots (EA's own random pick), and EA's post-route steps run as after a successful route: the `NumSuccess` tally (unless `HigherPriorityNext` / `UserCanceled` is set), `beforeEntering` (false = EA's `RouteOutside` from the door and false), then `AnimateEnterRabbitHole(a, slot, doRoute, meta)`: door, enter animation, fade, hide position, in-rabbit-hole posture, events. `doRoute` is false when the Sim still stands on the slot, true when `beforeEntering` moved it (EA's own short route back). Only the Sim's start must be unseen; a door in view is allowed only when nothing runs between the move and the enter animation (no `beforeEntering`, or the base no-op), and the Sim is then faded out first. Not ours (EA's code runs, counted): no proxy or slots, followers, not standing / held, seen or out of scope, a door in view with a custom `beforeEntering`, an exit reason set, the walk-time hold given up. Counters [1441-1452].
- `rhoutside` (tuning `kRhOutside`): `RabbitHole.RouteOutside` (the 7-argument overload) plans a whole trip to 2-4 m outside an entrance slot. Callers that go there first (EA's eat-outside interaction, and other mods' interactions that call it) fail before `RouteNearEntranceAndEnterRabbitHole`, so the rabbit-hole shortcut never sees those trips. An unseen lone standing townie far from a building whose door is unseen is placed at a `SafeSpot` on the building's lot near that slot, then EA's `RouteOutside` runs unchanged and plans only the last metres. Counters [1453-1455].

Self-check: `node tools/selftest/rabbitholedirect/run.ts` (the gate and the route choice).

Evidence [counters re-read from the ts3native logs of 109 local games, 2026-11-15/16, Bridgeport apartment, Sunset Valley, Twinbrook, Isla Paradiso, switches on, other local switches also on]: `rabbitholedirect` asked 2-940 times a game and entered 2-916 (55,058 of 57,334 in all); the enter animation failed 8 times in all, `beforeEntering` moved the Sim once, a door in view was faded 305 times. `rhoutside` was called 9-1,498 times a game and placed 2-461 (19,622 of 84,401 in all; the calls count the switch off too). An earlier run pair [not re-read] showed EA's rabbit-hole plans 129 to 73 (Bridgeport) and 309 to 151 (Sunset Valley), failed plans 4 to 1 and 8 to 1.

Not measured: a clean on/off A/B with no other rabbit-hole mod installed. `rhoutside` was built for a third-party mod's eat-outside interaction that calls `RouteOutside`; how much of its traffic is EA-only is unknown.

Soak 2026-11-19 [community-reported, Dalexlol, PR #507 comment]: 17 fresh loads (etl14k-etl14p, 6 worlds, 493.5 min, Apex + bridge), all 17 PASS, no crash, no access violation; both switches on. `rabbitholedirect`: asked 10,945, entered 7,667 (17 jobs), followers 392, posture or held 286, seen or scope 304, beforeEntering said no 13, exit reason or hold given up 2,230, enter animation failed or threw 11 (6 jobs, returned as a failed entry), faded 0. `rhoutside`: calls 14,796, placed 2,700, no safe spot 17. No script error has a frame from RabbitHoleDirect.cs. No off arm in the same load: it acts and keeps runs clean, saving not measured. Household: `IsPlayerSim`, household null and `TownieUnseen` gates in RhDirect / RhOutside (followers, riding, seen Sims, world lots and Sims already on the lot keep EA's trip) [inferred from the diff, rule review 2026-11-20].
