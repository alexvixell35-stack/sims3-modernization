## Three findings to start from

1. A T4 elder never dies of old age: with no Sim, `AgingManager.AgeTransitionWithoutCake` only cancels alarms.
   Either keep elders near the end out of T4, or guard that method (promote first, or EA's description-level
   `KillSim.KillSimExecute`).
2. A household switch instantiates missing members directly and leaves a base placeholder behind. Our instantiate
   hook and load-time cleanup must destroy our placeholder (rule P4).
3. Careers and school of a Sim with no object stand still (no pay, performance, grades). Safe, but not vanilla over
   days: career drift (FS-18) is needed for T4, and it must not call EA's level setters on a bare description.
