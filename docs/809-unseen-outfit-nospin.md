# 809: `unseenoutfitnospin`, an unseen townie's spin outfit change takes the WithoutSpin path (default off, needs `unseenoutfitdefer`)

Answer: `Sim.SwitchToOutfitWithSpin(SwitchOutfitHelper, bool)` is the funnel of every spin outfit change. It starts a delayed native build at once and waits for its ready callback, so `unseenoutfitdefer` (docs/586, PR #457) cannot hold it. With `unseenoutfitnospin` on (tuning `kUnseenOutfitNoSpin`, off by default, only with `unseenoutfitdefer` on), an unseen townie's spin change takes the result of EA's own `SwitchToOutfitWithoutSpin` instead: the managed state changes at once and the native set is a plain one, which `unseenoutfitdefer` then holds until the Sim can be seen.

- What the funnel does and what is kept: the helper's fields are left as after EA's `ChangeOutfit` (started false, ready true, category 0), the laundry call is made with the same arguments, the Sim ends in the state `DoOutfitDelayedSwap` leaves (visible outfit, revealing score, censor, body shape, `PostOutfitSwap`). The Sim skips the spin animation and the build wait, as an unseen Sim with EA's WithoutSpin changes does. Details in the comment of `UnseenOutfitNoSpin.cs`.
- Not taken (EA's funnel runs, counted by reason [3538-3544]): a helper started, aborted, disposed or active, no change, not standing or carrying a child or under 'child', a transform buff / supernatural form / ghost / age test / no stored outfit, a category outside `unseenoutfitdefer`'s safe set, a pending outfit, an open delayed swap or a Sim's first set after instantiate, a Sim that is seen or within the defer's margin. Household, selected and unknown-scope Sims are never taken.
- Counters [3537-3546].

Evidence [counters re-read from the ts3native logs of 58 local games with the switch on, 2026-11-16: Bridgeport apartment 28, Sunset Valley 24, Isla Paradiso 3, Twinbrook 3]: taken 42-405 a game (14,157 in all); refused for posture / age / animation 1,014, form or no stored outfit 551, category 307, pending / open / fresh 812, seen or near 633, helper 4, no change 2; 0 sets ran at once, 0 checks threw. Not measured: the outfit work saved. An earlier 6-game comparison of CAS compositor ms per minute showed no visible effect (Bridgeport 3,416 vs 3,944 and 2,924 on other runs; Twinbrook 2,646 vs 3,386 and 2,453; machine-load verdicts of part of those runs were invalid) [not re-read].
