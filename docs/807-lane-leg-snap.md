# 807: `unseenlanesnap`, `unseenlegsnap`, an unseen townie's portal or vehicle leg ends without a replan (ON since import 2026-11-20)

Answer: two ts3fixes switches (ON since import 2026-11-20) skip EA's mid-route replan for an unseen townie when the replan only moves the route's start point to where the Sim already is. Household, selected and unknown-scope Sims, and Sims in view, always get EA's replan, and nothing changes with the switch off.

- Cause [from three Bridgeport apartment games, routefail trace "prev" field, not re-read for this PR]: 70 % of the unseen townies' main-thread mid-route replans are EA's portal fix-up replan. A portal action (elevator cab, door, gate, stairs, pool ladder) returns `ContinueAndPopPathAndReplan` when the Sim came out of another lane than the planned one (the elevator assigns each rider a cab slot). `RoutingComponent.DoRoute` then replans the rest from the Sim on its own task: a sync plan of about 70 ms in the towers whatever its length. A car or boat leg that ends away from the planned exit does the same.
- `unseenlanesnap` (tuning `kUnseenLaneSnap`, `LaneSnap.cs`, `LaneSnapCore.cs`): for an unseen townie the hooked `PerformAction` of `PortalAction` and `PortalTraversalAction` turns that result into plain `ContinueAndPopPath`, after putting the Sim on the next path's start (the planned lane's exit of the same portal): routable, not below ground or in a pool, out of view, on a docked houseboat if any. Within 0.5 m nothing moves. Counters [1456-1470].
- `unseenlegsnap` (tuning `kUnseenLegSnap`, `RouteSources.cs` `LegSnap`): the same for a car or boat leg end that `legreplanskip` leaves because it is more than its near band away. The vehicle stays where it stopped, as after EA's replan. Counters [1471-1475].
- Kept for the game: forced replans, walk-style restarts, killed or forcibly replanned actions, pets and horses, riders, carried or carrying Sims, slaves, swimmers, a next path that is not a walk (an elevator's own continuation counts as one), and a path 1 that does not start where the portal path was planned to end.

Self-check: `node tools/selftest/lanesnap/run.ts` (102 checks of the decision order).
