# 805: `stagedarrival`, `icecreamdirect`, `nearvenue`, how a townie's trip is chosen and ended (default off)

Answer: three default-off ts3fixes switches change what an unseen townie (or an unseen ice cream truck) does on a trip. All three leave household, selected and unknown-scope Sims alone and do nothing with the switch off.

- `stagedarrival` (tuning `kStagedArrival`): the trip shortcuts (lot visits, GoHome, rabbit holes) refuse a trip whose destination is in view or near the household, so EA runs the whole walk. With the switch on, the unseen townie is placed at the street point nearest to the destination that is out of view (first on the straight line back to the Sim every 10 m, then the street side of lots near that line, in the destination's flood colour, routable for the Sim), after the game's own leaving lead-in; EA then plans only the last stretch, which the player sees as a normal arrival. A GoHome to an apartment shell keeps EA's walk. A Sim staged twice within 10 sim-minutes walks. Counters [3455-3475], list in `TripStage.cs`.
- `icecreamdirect` (tuning `kIceCreamDirect`): `IceCreamTruck.OneShotRouteToLot` plans a full car route on the sim thread to a street parking matrix, drives it, then parks (or fades out and is destroyed when ending service). A truck that is not seen where it stands, whose parking matrix and lot are not seen, and whose destination is not the active household's lot, skips the plan and the drive and runs EA's own after-drive branch at once. Only the two ends are tested, not the road between them. With the switch off the hook only counts (no random roll, no reservation). Counters [3476-3485].
- `nearvenue` (tuning `kNearVenue`): EA scores a community lot for a visit with no distance term, so a townie crosses the map, or the water, for a venue type that is also next door. With the switch on the score of a community lot is multiplied by a gravity factor (1/(distance + water penalty)^2 against the other open lots of the same venue type, normalised so the group keeps its summed score, clamped to 0.1-4). Household, selected, no-household Sims, Sims with a role or in a situation keep EA's score. Counters [3486-3495].

Self-check: `node tools/selftest/tripstage/run.ts` (candidate spacing, lot-entry candidates, colour ring, ice cream decision order, gravity factor).

Evidence [counters re-read from 145 ts3native logs of local games, 2026-11-15/16, `icecreamdirect` off]: the truck hook was asked 1-57 times a game (4,246 in all) and would have skipped 2,133 of them (50 %). The switch itself was off in every one of those games, so the skip path and its effect have no counters. `stagedarrival` and `nearvenue` ran only on 2026-11-14, before counter dumps carried their slots: no counters can be re-read.
