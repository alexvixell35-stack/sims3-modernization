## 1. `unseentraffic` [verified: scripts; measured: plan cost]

Ambient traffic is `LightweightTaxi` (EP3), the only `CarNpcWithNoDriver`. `TrafficManager` spawns one per lot class up to its cap at its check (0.5-1 sim h) and the car drives around for 0.5-1 h. Its LiteDriver task loops: random commercial lot, parking spot, a car plan and `DoRoute` when over 10 m away, free the spot, sleep 5-10 sim minutes; at the end `DriveAwayInNpcCar` waits, picks an off-screen road point and plans once more. Each plan cost 0.4-1.1 s of async route time on Bridgeport (local routetrace).

With the switch: (A) at the spot ask (LiteDriver's own call, spot granted), when the car, the spot and the straight corridor between them (a sample every 50 m, capped at 60) are unseen, nobody is within 4 m of the spot and the car is not leaving, the car is placed at the spot with EA's `Car.PlaceAt` and the task sleeps the leg's drive time (1-90 sim minutes) with the spot reserved; EA's loop then does its arrival steps (within 10 m: no plan, free the spot, sleep). (B) at drive-off an unseen car skips the wait and the plan: the spot is freed as EA's `finally` does, then EA's `Release` and `Destroy` (or `DisposeSpreadDefer` with `disposespread`). A seen car, spot or corridor, or any occupant, runs EA's code. "Seen" = camera reach, within 80 m of an active-household Sim, or on screen. Only the one car class is affected; carpool, taxi, school bus, service cars, trucks and household cars never reach these hooks. No Sim is touched.

## 2. `npccarexit`

`ReleaseNpcCar` -> `DriveAwayInNpcCar` takes the first answer of `FindPlaceOnRoadOffScreen`; for the same start and camera it is always the same road point. Sunset Valley: 79 `FailedBecauseNoPathToDestination` plans in 4 games, all to one goal from one household street 164.5 m away [measured, local logs]. EA plans anyway, never checks the result and releases the car where it stands. With the switch the drive-away ask marks the exit pending; a car plan that fails NoPath to it teaches a memo (8 m start cell, 1 m goal cell); a later ask whose first answer is in the memo tries the other candidates, then one far re-ask, then EA's own "no place" branch (skips only the doomed plan). Household or selectable occupants and food trucks are exempt; tables capped (256 / 64) and cleared at world load.

## 3. Evidence

Neither switch was ever on in a game: the decisions are covered by the offline selftest (`UnseenTrafficCore`: corridor samples, hold time, call shape, memo keys). Deciding tests (not run): Bridgeport, one smoke game with `unseentraffic` on (counters 3801 / 3808 fire, no errors, cars still visible when the camera is near); Sunset Valley, one game with `npccarexit` on (counters 3822 / 3821, failed ReleaseNpcCar plans fall), then an in-load A/B each.
