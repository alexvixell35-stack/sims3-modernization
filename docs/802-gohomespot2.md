# 802: `gohomespot2`, a second landing-spot search for the GoHome shortcut (default off)

Answer: the GoHome shortcut (`gohome`) places an unseen townie on its home lot through `SafeSpot`, which makes three fresh tries from one random outdoor point of the lot. When none finds a landing spot, EA runs the real trip (a walk or drive of up to hundreds of metres). With `gohomespot2` on (tuning `kGoHomeSpot2`, off by default), one more `SafeSpot` search starts from the home's mailbox (else its front door), a fixed and normally reachable origin, before the shortcut gives up.

- Cause [verified for the count: run gm4, Sunset Valley]: the three fresh tries start from the same random point, so a point in a walled-off corner, or one where `FindGoodLocation` answers nothing, fails all three. In Sunset Valley the shortcut found no spot in 100 of 1,041 GoHomes; EA then walked those Sims 140-480 m, and 21 of those walks were cut at the 250 ms own-plan deadline (`NoPathToDestination`) and replanned [inferred from the run notes, not re-read for this PR].
- Fix (`Fixes.cs`, `GoHomeHook.RunHook` and `GhSecondSpot`): the second search uses the same `SafeSpot` (on the lot, the `IsPointInLotSafelyRoutable` verdict and its other rules), so a found spot is as safe as a first-try one; the caller still applies the in-view test and the loop guard. An origin on another lot is refused.
- Scope: only reached from `GoHomeHook` for an unseen townie (`gohome` on and `Unseen`); household, selected and unknown-scope Sims never get here. Off: no extra call.
- Cost: a second search is up to three more `FindGoodLocation` calls plus verdict checks. Measured 405 ms in 87 searches in a Bridgeport game, 343 ms in 84 in a Sunset Valley game.
- Counters: [3424] second searches, [3425] found, [3426] ms spent.
- Evidence [verified for the counters: ts3native.log of gm6; the rest from the gm4 / gm6 comparison, natural-play games on the same saves, one machine, not in-load A/Bs]: second searches / found / ms: Bridgeport 87 / 60 / 405, Sunset Valley 84 / 66 / 343. "No spot" GoHome refusals (EA walked) fell from 90 to 27 in Bridgeport and from 100 to 18 in Sunset Valley; GoHome plans failing `NoPath` fell from 9 to 6 (Bridgeport) and from 20 to 18 (Sunset Valley). Other differences between gm4 and gm6 were not listed again for this PR.
